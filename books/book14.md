# Book 14 — The Lighthouse You Build (Self-efficacy + future vision)

Stepping onto rocks, the crew raised a lighthouse from driftwood and glass chanting rhyme loops: plan/scan, beam/dream, glow/flow. Rome sketched the frame, Rose set first brace, friends tied lace. Scheme turned corona—ABABBBCB—each line feeding next like waves feed light. When the lamp shone steady, they knew signal can come from hands that rhyme with will and sight.
