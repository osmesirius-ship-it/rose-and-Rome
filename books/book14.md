# Book 14 — The Lighthouse You Build (Self-efficacy + future vision)

Stepping onto rocks, the crew raised a lighthouse from driftwood and glass, chanting plan and scan. Rome sketched the frame, Rose set the brace, friends tied the lace. The beacon proved the Hermetic principle of creation: thought becomes thing when effort follows vision. Their rhyme loop circled like waves feeding light, each verse a plank, each chorus a bolt, until the tower shone steady. They learned that signals can come from their own hands and that shared work turns hope into glow. They kept building and believing, repeating that steady practice and shared sight make guiding beams real.
