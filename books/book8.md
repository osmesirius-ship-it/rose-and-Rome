# Book 8 — The Switchback Path (Mental flexibility + reframing)

Inside the switchback trail the crew traced every bend and whispered that each turn carried a choice. Rome mapped zigzags and taught that changing direction was not failure but adaptation. Rose practiced holding two pictures at once: the cliff seen from below and the same cliff seen from above. Together they named reframing as a form of kindness, a way to release fear by seeing new steps. They repeated the Hermetic principles of polarity and correspondence, noticing how every inside worry had an outside match and how a small change in thought reshaped the road ahead.
