# Book 9 — The Echoing Well (Listening + internal voice)

Through the stone well, the crew tested echo spells that rhymed on rolling O: glow/slow, below/hello. Rome sent calm lines and heard them doubled back in flow. Rose tossed a harsh remark, felt sting regrow, so she rewrote speech to be seed not blow. The lattice widened—AABB with echo ends—teaching that what we drop returns, so plant words and let inner chamber grow!!
