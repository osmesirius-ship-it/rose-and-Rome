# Book 9 — The Echoing Well (Listening + internal voice)

They found an echoing well that repeated any word dropped into it, and Rose said, let’s try kind words first. The well answered softly, and the crew smiled, because their own voices sounded like friends. Rome explained that the inner voice is like a small well too, and we choose what we drop in. If we say, I can try, the echo says, you can try; if we say, I am safe, it says, you are safe. They learned the Hermetic rule of vibration in simple terms: words are little waves, and waves touch our bodies. Their rhyme now used gentle repeats, like echoes, making the tune feel calm. They promised to speak softly inside, so the echoes would carry light for all.
At noon they played a listening game, where one friend spoke and the others waited, then repeated the message with care. Rose said, waiting is a kind of respect, and Rome said, when we listen, we hear our own hearts too. The well taught them that harsh words echo back, so they chose gentle ones for the day. Their rhyme grew into a chain of repeating sounds, with the start of each line matching the end of the last, a small puzzle that still felt easy. They practiced saying, I’m learning, I’m growing, I’m here, and the well answered, here, here, here. The crew understood that kind echoes make a safe circle, and a safe circle helps everyone hear truth with calm joy.
In the evening they told the well a secret wish, and it returned the wish in a bright, clear voice, like a tiny star. Rose said, as above so below, the stars listen too, and Rome said, as within so without, our own words can light our way. They practiced a slightly trickier rhyme, with echoes nested inside each line, yet it still felt like a lullaby. When worries tried to speak, the crew answered with kinder words, and the worries grew quiet. They went to sleep knowing the echo they feed their hearts will be the echo that returns. Tomorrow they would choose brave words again, because a gentle echo is a helper, not a haunt for kids and grownups alike too.
