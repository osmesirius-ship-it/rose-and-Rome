# Book 5 — Two Sides of the Same Sky (Polarity + self-compassion)

Rose said, “I’m angry.” Rome said, “That makes sense.”
She said, “And I miss them.” He said, “Also true—hence
You can want love and hate what you got.
You can hurt and be brave. That’s allowed. That’s a lot.”

Junie asked softly, “Can sad days still end?”
Rome nodded. “A feeling is not your whole friend—
It visits. It lingers. It leaves when it can.
We don’t have to worship it. That’s the plan.”

“Two things can be true on one tough little day:
It hurt—and you’re healing, in your own brave way.”

And Rose, for the first time, didn’t pick just one truth—
She held both like lanterns and walked into youth.
