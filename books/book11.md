# Book 11 — The Threads Between (Community + interdependence)

Among patchwork blankets the crew stitched squares with interlaced rhyme—thread/red, quilt/tilt, proof/roof—braiding mutual proof. Rome stitched a compass, Rose a spark, Junie a joke that lit dark. Scheme shifted to ABABCB, weaving lines leaning on what came before. When storms rattled roof, they pulled cloth and felt the chorus core: warmth comes from hands in lore.
