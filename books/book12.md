# Book 12 — The Weather inside the Room (Emotional literacy + naming states)

Though skies were clear, the crew mapped feelings as weather: fog/log, heat/beat, hail/veil. Rome said “Foggy,” Rose “Lightning in ribs,” Timo “Drizzle with sun.” Their stanza braided ABCBDE, each name leaning on the last rung. Naming made forecasts: blanket for cold, breath for thunder, rest for spun gusts. No cloud stayed forever; joy moved too, and both were sung.
