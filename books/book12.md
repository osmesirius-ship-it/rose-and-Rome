# Book 12 — The Weather inside the Room (Emotional literacy + naming states)

Though skies outside were clear, the crew mapped feelings as weather. Rome said foggy, Rose said lightning in ribs, Timo said drizzle with sun. Naming gave them forecasts: blankets for cold fear, breath for thunder, rest for dizzy gusts. They repeated the Hermetic truth that motion never stops; feelings change like clouds. Their rhyme pattern layered different weathers in sequence, teaching that every state deserves respect and that storms pass. They named each inner cloud, repeating that awareness and preparation let courage stay gentle and steady.
