# Book 12 — The Weather inside the Room (Emotional literacy + naming states)

Rose brought weather cards with suns, clouds, rain, and wind, and she asked the crew to name their feelings like the sky. Rome said, if I feel stormy, I can say stormy, and that helps me breathe. They learned that naming a feeling is like turning on a light, and the room feels less scary. The Hermetic idea of correspondence appeared again: as above so below, the sky outside and the sky inside can match. Their rhyme now used a simple forecast pattern, with sounds that shifted like weather, a little more complex, yet still easy to hear, because it matched the day. They practiced saying, my sky is cloudy now, but it can clear, and that gave hope to all of us.
At noon the weather inside the room changed, and the kids shared it without fear: one felt breezy, one felt rainy, one felt bright. Rose said, feelings move like fronts, so we can plan and care for each other. Rome added, as within so without, when we name the sky inside, our outside choices get kinder. They made a rhyme with small storms and calm ends, a pattern that folded back on itself, yet it still felt gentle, like a cozy forecast. When someone felt thunder, the group offered a blanket and a story, and the thunder softened to rain. They learned that no weather lasts forever, and the sun always finds a way to peek through for the tired hearts inside us.
By evening they drew a weather map of the day, and each child placed a star on the moment they felt better. Rose said, the map shows change, and change means we can wait out hard moments. Rome said, this is the law of rhythm in kid words: feelings rise and fall, like tides and clouds. Their rhyme now braided three sounds in a calm loop, a bit more complex, yet still clear, because each line named the weather first. They thanked the day for teaching them to speak their skies, and they went to sleep knowing storms pass and mornings return. Tomorrow they would check the forecast of their hearts and help one another pack umbrellas for any rainy moments too again.
