# Book 13 — The Key of Quiet Questions (Curiosity + consent)

Easing toward a locked door, Rose knocked and asked, letting rhyme spiral ABCBCA: key/agree, ask/task, turn/return. Rome said questions are music; they open only when another hand joins. Crew practiced call-and-response—“Want help?” “Can I sit close?”—yes chimed brass, no rang glass. Consent shaped the verse and respect kept cadence burn/turn, keeping doorway theirs.
