# Book 6 — The Tide and the Tempo (Rhythm + riding the ups/downs)

In stormy mornings the crew watched sea breathe, counting rush and hush to stay on beat. Rome clapped patterns and Rose set steps, leaning with crest then dip so neither froze. They rode swell not grind, humming through dread until calm aligned. Soft rhyme repeats: flow, grow. Rhythms keep balance when feelings come and go, and turning with tide keeps courage in row.
