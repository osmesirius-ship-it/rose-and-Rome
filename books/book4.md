# Book 4 — The Humming Chest (Vibration + emotional regulation)

Some nights, Rose’s heart went drum-drum in her chest,
Like it wanted to sprint though her feet needed rest.
Rome said, “That’s your body on lookout for wrong—
It learned it the hard way. It learned it for long.”

He taught her a trick with a slow counting beat:
“Name five things you see. Feel both soles of your feet.”
Then hum like a motor that idles, not flies—
And watch how the shaking unhooks from your eyes.

“Feelings can wobble; they don’t have to win.
Breathe in the steady—breathe out the spin.”

And even when storms came and stars couldn’t show,
Rose felt her own steadiness start up and glow.
