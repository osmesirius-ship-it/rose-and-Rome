# Book 10 — The Lantern’s Ledger (Accountability + repair)

Into a lantern ledger the crew wrote faults and fixes, proving that naming harm creates light. Rose confessed when she snapped and also recorded how she patched the pane. Rome admitted forgetting the watch and wrote his plan to stand next time. The ledger braided the Hermetic law of cause and effect with daily choices, reminding them that repair is an action not a wish. Each rhyme in their chant paired mistake with mending, showing how trust returns when effort returns. They circled back to amends, repeating that honesty, action, and shared glow rebuild safety together.
