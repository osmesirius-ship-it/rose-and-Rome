# Book 10 — The Lantern’s Ledger (Accountability + repair)

Into a lantern ledger they wrote faults and fixes with braided rhyme—note/float, mend/blend, debt/let. Rose confessed, “I snapped,” “I patched the pane.” Rome marked, “Forgot the watch,” “I stirred the stew.” The scheme turned AAB BCC, a climb that held both ache and new. The glow proved that naming harm then weaving repair lets trust accrue and shine back through!!!
