# Book 7 — The Mirror and the Many (Gender + shared strengths)

Notes in Junie’s mirror shimmered chores and charms: Rose fixing hinges while Rome braided hair, Timo tending flame, Lei guarding air. Labels that haunted like ghosts lost force when roles re-spun in playful pairs. The rhyme stepped up: share, care, dare, repair. Each child could mend or lead or soothe; the couplets showed Many mirror one light, threads twined fair!!
